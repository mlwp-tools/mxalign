# Spatial alignment

A detailed guide to `ds.mx.align_space_with` and `mx.align_space`. It will
cover the grid layouts mxalign understands (flattened `grid_index`, regular
latitude/longitude, projected `xc`/`yc`), how to attach a projection with
`add_crs` / `add_grid_mapping`, the grid-to-grid identity check, and what
happens to points outside the grid.
